# Calibration

Daily signals are study inputs, not predictions of examination content. Each working hypothesis must retain supporting evidence, counterevidence, and a confidence strictly between 0 and 1.

## Weekly review

1. Compare selected signals with rejected quota, duplicate, access-policy, and source-degraded records.
2. Review next-day feedback for false relevance, missed institutions, weak mappings, and overconfident wording.
3. Sample citations and verify that the quoted evidence still matches the recorded content SHA-256.
4. Compare 337/902 mapping reasons with the versioned `ZJU-IDI-2027` sections.
5. Lower confidence when sources are correlated, metadata-only, or missing methods.
6. Change a selection or mapping rule only with before/after fixture tests and a documented reason.

## Confidence guide

- `0.50-0.59`: limited or metadata-only support; use as a question prompt.
- `0.60-0.74`: multiple fields and provenance align; still a working hypothesis.
- `0.75-0.90`: strong primary-source and method support. Never express certainty.

The deterministic fixture is for schema, UI, and operational calibration only. It cannot improve a real-world hypothesis confidence score.
